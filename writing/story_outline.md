# Tank Rampage Command - Story Outline

## Brief Notes

**Narrative Goal**: Humanity is just beginning to fight against the rogue AI. The AI is initially somewhat inadequate, but can put up a fight. Over time, it will learn and make the battle more difficult (thereby increasing gameplay difficulty).

- Comment from Justin:
> Responding to: Maybe this is very early in the war and humanity is winning before the AI gets the upper hand. So the player can win up until the end but the narrative hints at a struggle to come?
> I like a version of the last one [see above] since this is a prequel and humanity still has a strong army to mount a defense.  Humanity has upper hand in beginning but as AI learns from battles it gets stronger and battles become more difficult

- We know that by the time Artillery Rampage / Tank Rampage happen, the AI has become suffocatingly powerful. However, during the TRC timeline humanity has a fighting chance. Gameplay narrative should reinforce that humans are winning, but the fight becomes increasingly more difficult. The player won't be "losing" their battles, but winning the overall war may start to feel hopeless.
  - In a perfect world, TRC would have moments where the player legitimately feels surprised by the AI's tactics. Such as:
    - pull a bait-and-switch
    - lay an ambush
    - set up a small outpost out of sight used to attack the player after the player invests all their resources attacking a "main" base
