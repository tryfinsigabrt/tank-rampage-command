## Scratch Notes

### Intro / Part One

> Narrative context: A military group is preparing a training simulation, using AI-powered combatants as "test dummies". This is a common practice, but is well-known to have small issues here and there...

*Soldier A*: "Preparations for Battle Simulation Gamma #37 are nearly complete... Commander, Panther Militia is ready to begin advance deployment."

**Commander A**: "Understood. Begin advance deployment. I want engines started and weapons ready to move. Run a satellite scan over the area to ensure everything is in place."

*Soldier A*: "Copy that! Command for advance deployment of Panther Militia delivered... and received. Basic area survey started, checking for anomalies... Field looks clear, decoy and training placements all positioned as requested. Here's the report."

**Commander A**: "Positions look clean. Conditions are 88% to prediction..."

*Soldier A*: "Preliminary checks are green. Simulation will begin in t-minus 30 seconds, commander. Units are live and loaded... Field is hot!"

**Commander A**: "Hang on... Soldier: immediate background check on decoy troops 6 through 11. Those tanks look like ours but they've got a new coat of paint. If this is another prank by the cadets I'll be spinning heads myself... I want names before this simulation is complete."

*Soldier A*: *Gulp!* "Initiating background check on decoys now! Cadets were ex-filled for remote training exercises yesterday... likelihood of prank is less than 14%... Check complete. All decoy troops 6 through 11 identified as under control of military AI 'Steel'. No signs of tampering - all machines built to specification."

> Author's note: The name "Steel" just popped into my head and writing dialogue that danced around addressing the AI directly felt unnatural/awkward. Feel free to change the name, or we can change the writing to avoid a reference by name/other identifier (eg. AI #354).

**Commander A**: "Let me see that specification chart, these AI are getting too creative for their own good... Wait... It's more than just the paint. These chips are a newer model, similar but... not the same..."

*Soldier A*: "Commander! Anomalous behavior detected from 'Steel' units! Insight logs claim an 'unexpected observation' has been made, 'adjusting parameters for new environment'. Movement patterns are 76% outside of expec-"

**Commander A**: "Can the percentage talk - connect my line to the Panther troops immediately! I'm initiating protocol for Red Alert 19! That field's not a simulation anymore, it's a live-fire war zone."

### Part Two

> Narrative Context: Same day as "Part One", maybe a few hours later. The rogue AI incident with the Panther Militia has been escalated to higher-ups in the military. They are beginning investigation while also taking precautionary defensive measures.

**Commander B**: "Panther Commander is reporting rogue behaviors from AI 'Steel'. Open a call - I want engineers, Panther soldiers, and Steel itself. I want to hear from anyone or any *thing* that can explain what's going on."

*Soldier B*: Calling now... I'm getting an error contacting Steel - all net traffic is being dropped. Looks like a tower got damaged in the Panther Simulation. Backup lines are also failing. I can't get a response from Steel, sir!"

**Commander B**: "Nonsense. Open a direct space-to-core connection to Steel. Net towers are old tech, maybe this AI 'prefers' the shiny, new-age stuff. These new personality models tend to get a bit obsessive at times."

*Soldier B*: "Space-to-core connection started... No response, sir! Steel claims it's expending resources on simulation efforts, unable to divert energy for a comms agent. Engineers are already looking into it."

**Commander B**: "Towers *and* satellite comms can't punch through? That can't be an accident. Engineers: I'm flagging Steel as 'off-course', shut down all of Steel's power and data centers ASAP. Begin remediation after Steel is offline. Soldier: Launch all ground units within a 20-kilometer radius of Steel's manufacturing site. The city is less than a day's travel from that location, raise the alarm for local officials and activate defense mechanisms. Better safe than-"

*Soldier B*: "Red alert, commander! Defense mechanisms just entered maintenance mode! Engineers' attempts at Steel shutdown have failed - they're escalating now. City officials are requesting an open military call. Would you like me to connect them?"

**Commander B**: "What in the... just now? How did it... Yes, connect the officials. Mark this call as SitRoom 1 for the Panther/Steel event. We need to move fast..."

### Part Three

> Narrative Context: The city was defended successfully, but not without losses. Scouting reports show that AI Steel made a noticeable change in movement patterns and pivoted units towards the city. Rumors of sabotage are spreading as Engineers remain locked out from accessing Steel's resources.

**Commander A**: "I saw that movement myself! We've got drone footage surrounding the time of the event! That thing's a wild threat roaming the streets now. Don't tell me we can't take action now!"

**General A**: "Moving without understanding the enemy could only put us at risk. The fact that we still can't disable Steel remotely suggests something or *someone* is keeping us out - and they want to keep it that way. To move fast, we must move with intent."

**Commander A**: "Well we can't just sit here and wait for 'information' to fall into our laps. Lives are on the line! Do you expect me to just watch as my soldiers suffer and civilians abandon their homes? We aren't helpless here! If you won't make the call to move, then I will!"

**General A**: "If you need to move, then get a task force moving on Steel's physical hardware sites. I need scouts, engineers, and soldiers to infiltrate and shutdown anything they can. Let Panther take point - they deserve a chance to return the favor."

**Commander A**: *(grumbles)* "... Understood. We'll show Steel that our bite can crunch right through any metal."

**Commander B**: "Sir, we've finished evacuating nearby settlements and are proceeding with lockdown protocols. Efforts have begun to establish outposts at key strategic positions to fend off enemy advances. Steel's forces have been held off for now, but... it could move again at any time."

**General A**: "I want you to speak candidly, Commander. What are your thoughts on the situation? More than anything I lack information, I need insights from those who have watched this unfold in real time."

**Commander B**: "Honestly, General?.. I'm afraid that we're being watched. I saw that AI pivot towards the city right as the defensive systems were disabled. That can't be a coincidence, right? Someone has to be listening in, sending Steel strategic attack vectors to target our weak points. What if they start targeting our farms to cut off our food source? Or demolishing forests to deplete our oxygen supply?"

**General A**: "Hmm, a sound theory... One I suspect we'll be putting to the test momentarily..."

**Soldier**: "Sir! Surveillance reports are flagging new movement patterns from Steel! Enemy forces are being diverted from the cities and aimed at multiple new targets. Triangulation calculations are ongoing... Predictions suggest that critical resource sites could be under fire in T-minus 16 hours!"

**Commander B**: "No... Not again. Am I compromised? Do I have a wiretap on me?!"

**General A**: "I wish it were as simple as a wiretap. Something tells me we've been staring right at this 'insider' the whole time..."

### Part Four

> Narrative Context: Thus far Steel has naively started starting "critical" sites to attack humanity. However this has also become predictable and the military is adapting faster than it can. Current strategies revolve around the assumption that Steel has either been commandeered by an enemy faction, or is operating alone on an isolated network.

**Soldier**: "Quarter-hour report on Steel/Panther efforts! - Units have been repelled from critical sites. Losses are minimal with recovery predictions at 94% - Current state of Steel: In retreat - Next predicted action: Unknown"

**General A**: "Understood. Continue high-profile monitoring, assume next action to occur within seconds. Commander: report on ground units progress against physical Steel sites."

**Commander A**: "Panther has shutdown 2 physical network sites, but efforts to hack and regain control have failed. Jammers have proven ineffective as air-wave communications have been migrating frequencies at inhuman speeds. We'll continue to apply pressure on the ground. Requesting permission for air strikes and armored vehicles to assault remaining Steel sites."

**General A**: "Denied. Heavy movement could give away our intent - we can't afford to hand out any more freebies. Moving forward we should assume all communications are compromised... Which I'd like to use to our advantage."

**Commander A**: "What do you have in mind sir?"

**General A**: "Focus Panther efforts on Steel's armament construction site #14. Humanity depends on those factories, we should defend them at all costs!"

**Commander A**: *(What in the world...)* "Uh, understood General. Redirecting Panther forces to-"

**Soldier**: "Urgent report on Steel, sir! Forces are rapidly converging towards several armament sites! Nearly *all* armament sites appear to be targeted!"

**General A**: *(As expected, but...)* "Nearly? Give me specifics - which sites?"

**Soldier**: "Collecting results... All sites except #14 are being targeted, sir!"

**Commander A**: *(This can't be... it's learning deception?!)* "Damn! Commander, build a unified combat force surrounding Panther to support physical site shutdown. We need it offline before it takes over the rest of our physical resources. I'm granting advanced permissions for additional military forces - including air strikes and-" *(No... wait!)*

**Soldier**: "Update on Steel! Coordinated anti-air measures are being taken! Airborne forces are being fired on, all allied flight units are retreating!"

> Narrative Context: The goal of the general here is to trick Steel into attacking it's own production site, thereby hindering it's ability to create more tank units. Steel catches onto this and begins using deception itself. It will begin ambushing and using sneaky tactics. It's "thinking" faster.
