# Tank Rampage Command - Story Brainstorm

## Lore notes

- Existing lore from Artillery/Tank Rampage
- An advanced AI has taken over the world by siphoning resources, building war machines (ie. tanks), etc.
  - "...salted the fields..."
  - "...simultaneous strike on all world leaders..."
- Opening dialogue sequence in Artillery Rampage really makes things seem dire, as though humanity is already on the brink of losing.
- TRC is a PRE-quel, so what's the story before AR & TR?
  - Obvious answer feels like telling the story of the AI's assault on the planet.
  - If the story has already established that the AI has ruined food supplies and attacked world powers, how do we reinforce a player narrative where they can "win"?
  - Idea: **What if we sneakily make it so that the player is actually controlling the AI?** The player's wins would actually be the AI winning battles against humanity.

## Elements

- By default, I assume a player would assume they would control "Humanity" in a "Humanity vs AI" game. But I noticed that currently the enemy has humanoid marine units. If we are to assume this battle is "Humanity vs AI", then the AI (whom the player is presumably fighting against) having human soldiers doesn't make as much sense.
- HOWEVER, if we twist the narrative slightly we can say the AI has created robotic android soldiers that mimic what the AI has learned from humanity. (Doesn't feel like too much of a stretch if we are already in a time where AI is manufacturing tanks and have begun to "feel" for themselves)
  - Narratively, all the combat barks and such would be voice recordings from human soldiers that the android soldiers simply repeat. Hence why the same voice lines keep playing over and over with little-to-no variance.
- The gameplay can reinforce the AI winning against humanity via the player simply winning each level. But telling the story of "winning" will be tricky unless the AI itself is the narrator - which feels kinda weird... Instead, what if the AI hacked into radio comms across the globe and is now listening in to the war strategy from humanity?
  - Dialogue and text presented to the player would represent hacked communications from humanity, and the AI is using that information to strategize and make decisive strikes.
- The player would be none the wiser about this. **We would never tell the player they are controlling the AI** (until maybe the end with a "shock" reveal). Instead we simply put the text in front of the player and give the impression that they are working on behalf of the people they hear from. But secretly the player is the AI - silently listening in to gain intel.

## Writing samples/ideas

### Level One - First Assault (Opening dialogue sequence, similar to Artillery Rampage)
- Humanity observes that an unknown force of tanks has begun coalescing near XYZ area. Does anyone know of a stockpile or training exercise going on today? No?.. Is this an invasion?! *Panic ensues*
- Emergency defensive measures are invoked!

#### Gameplay
The player leads a force of tanks against an opposing force and wins the fight. Victory for AI
  - The AI built a collection of tanks and was finally noticed. The AI itself panics and sends the tanks toward the area called out in the radio message.

---

### Level Two - Defend the city
- Radio messages from across the world are flaring up like wildfire. There are even more attacks happening all over!
- We (humanity) must assist our allies against this invasive force.
- Protecting civilians takes priority. Defend the city while everyone evacuates!

#### Gameplay 2
The player battles within a city to defeat a force of foot soldiers + tanks. Victory for AI in a populated area.
  - The AI hears about "the city" and decides to attack there.

---

### Level Three - Securing resources
- There are rumors spreading that the attacking force is a single entity making a coordinated attack. Further rumors suggest that an AI has gone haywire and begun attacking humans.
  - *Has the tech really advanced that far? How did we miss this?*
- Supply and trade routes are already being cutoff. We need to secure our crops and farmlands! If we lose our food, we won't have the fuel to fight back.

####  Gameplay 3
The player fights over farmland (similar to Tank Rampage) to defeat enemies and "control the zone(s)". Victory for AI over food sources.
  - The AI learns that food sources are critical, so it begins efforts to control farmland territories.
  - Bonus: maybe a "boss" fight with an extra big/powerful tank? Reference to Tank Rampage.

---

### Level Four - United as one
- Humanity is putting up its best, but covering all the space is getting difficult. Cities are falling, lives are being lost, and chaos is beginning to take hold in people's hearts.
  - Some people have lost hope. Some are starting to side with the AI out of desperation, misguided faith, or even ill intent.
- Suddenly critical intel flies in! War machines are moving across the globe, particularly near the countries of power! This could be a coordinated attack to eliminate every world leader in one fell swoop.
  - We'll somehow have to spread ourselves even further. All of humanity must now band together to protect our leaders.
- Open communications with all human-led countries to begin discussing planet-wide strategies. Travel to their communication hubs if necessary to establish contact.

#### Gameplay 4
The player fights against multiple factions, each that potentially begin fighting each other as well. The player must control territory within each faction's area to win. Victory for AI over communication tactics.
  - The AI was established across the world for manufacturing purposes. Naturally this meant it had forces near the major world powers built up - that's where it was set up to begin with!
  - The AI learns that each of these major areas area within its range. It moves to cut off communication efforts across all factions at once. By controlling each zone, the AI isolates each world power from the rest of humanity.

---

### Level Five - Take off

- Some world leaders are onboard and already strategizing. But some have yet to respond...
  - *Did we lose them already?*
- Tanks have been the primary threat, but they won't be able to target vehicles in air-space. Large-scale evacuation efforts have already begun using space shuttles.
  - Civilians are being packed in, even more are turned away. Sheer desperation has gotten the best of everyone as riots break out.
- We've been commanded to prioritize getting the space shuttles safely into orbit. A final stand to leave a spark of hope behind. We need to move in now for a defensive position before the AI can.

#### Gameplay 5
The player first moves in to "control the zone" in an airfield with a space shuttle. After gaining control of the area, waves of enemies roll in from all angles. All enemies must be defeated while controlling the zones. At the very end, the shuttle begins take off.
  - The AI learns that the shuttles are humanity's last hope. It moves in to stop the shuttle launch and is attacked from all angles by enemies, forcing it to defend and defeat the human-driven tanks.
  - The AI's tanks just aren't enough to stop the shuttle itself. It decides that artillery would be more beneficial for attacking at long-range + aerial targets. It begins rapid manufacturing of artillery units.

---

### Final Level - You hang up first

- The AI has significantly increased its scale of artillery units. The tanks begin to be outmatched purely due to the numbers and the range advantage.
- Every communication to protect an area was swiftly attacked, as though the AI was monitoring their every move... Hang on, there's an extra line connected to the network. Does anyone know whose line this is? Who is this?! Identify yourse- *(the line gets cut)*
- Humanity finally learns that the AI has been listening this whole time. They've been giving away all their critical points of interest directly to the enemy.
  - *What if the AI had never heard all that intel? Would it have even known to attack those locations? Would humanity have had a chance?*
- Humanity responds by gathering all their artillery units together. Bunkers are built all around as humans gather in silence like snakes ready to strike.

#### Gameplay 6

The player must capture several radio stations and fend off groups of long-range artillery. Upon clearing the area, the AI has successfully captured a critical mass of radios and has majority control of communication systems.
  - Maybe battlefield involves fighting over a small mountain range where only artillery can fire projectiles over?
  - Maybe the cost of artillery units goes significantly down? Gives the player a final power fantasy rush of many strong, long-range units.

---

### Finale - DMZ

- Radio communications are shut down across the globe. Trust is shattered as fear drives the trigger finger of every soldier still standing.
- The world becomes eerily quiet... until a battle breaks out and the sound of unending artillery fire absorbs the senses. The first to move is the first to receive a bombardment of explosive ordnance.
- Yet hope still remains. Engineers have been hard at work developing countermeasures. Modifications to existing equipment to give humanity an edge. But will it be enough?

*FIN - story leads into Artillery Rampage*
